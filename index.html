    Thesis system prototype

    About this system

    This interface implements the web-based monitoring component of "A Random Forest-Based Geospatial Analysis of Urban Expansion and Buffer Zone Encroachment in Mount Makiling Using Sentinel-2 Imagery," a thesis presented to the College of Computer Studies, Laguna State Polytechnic University – Los Baños Campus.

    Prototype notice. This build is a front-end interface only. It does not hold a live connection to Google Earth Engine, Sentinel-2 imagery, or a Flask/FastAPI backend. Map layers and charts are generated from a deterministic model of directional urban pressure (weighted toward Calamba and Santo Tomas, per the population data in Chapter II) so the interface can be reviewed and tested end-to-end before the real classification pipeline is wired in. KPI values, the Buffer Zone Encroachment Index, forest transition hectares, and the Random Forest confusion matrices/accuracy figures on the Dashboard and Results pages reflect the team's actual Google Earth Engine outputs (Random Forest classifier, noise-filtered). The Map Viewer's visual layers and the Shannon's Entropy / 500m ring metric are still illustrative placeholders pending live Earth Engine Python API / REST backend integration.

    Research objectives

    1. Obtain and preprocess multi-temporal Sentinel-2 imagery of Mount Makiling for land use and forest change monitoring.
    2. Derive classification input variables from spectral bands and vegetation indices related to urbanization-driven forest change.
    3. Classify land cover into urban/non-urban and forest/non-forest classes using two independently trained Random Forest classifiers.
    4. Map the extent and location of forest change driven by nearby urban development, 2019–2025.
    5. Quantify urban sprawl and buffer zone encroachment using the Urban Expansion Rate, land cover transitions, and zone-level urban and forest statistics.
    6. Evaluate each classifier with accuracy, a confusion matrix, and the Kappa coefficient on a held-out validation split.

    Study area

    Mount Makiling Forest Reserve (core reserve, ~4,362 ha), managed by UPLB, spans Bay, Los Baños, and Calamba City in Laguna, and Santo Tomas in Batangas. The buffer zone shapefile provided by MCME/UPLB represents the combined protected extent (core reserve + surrounding buffer, ~5,870 ha total); the buffer ring specifically (~1,508 ha) was isolated by spatial difference against the core reserve boundary, after verifying via a 99.98% spatial overlap check that the provided buffer zone shapefile fully encompasses the core reserve.

    Planned data & tooling

    • Sentinel-2 L2A · Copernicus Data Space
    • Google Earth Engine (classification + indices)
    • QGIS (validation, cartography)
    • scikit-learn (accuracy assessment)
    • Flask / FastAPI backend
    • Leaflet.js + Chart.js front end

    Ethical & scope notes

    Only open, publicly licensed Copernicus data is used; no human subjects, field access, or personally identifiable information is involved. Socioeconomic and policy variables are out of scope — the study focuses on geospatial and environmental indicators. Results are intended to support, not replace, ground validation and formal DENR/LGU planning processes.
